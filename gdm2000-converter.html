What is GDM2000?

GDM2000 — the Geodetic Datum of Malaysia 2000 — is the national geodetic datum of Malaysia. It is realised on the GRS80 ellipsoid and replaced the older Kertau 1968 (Peninsular Malaysia) and Timbalai 1948 (Sabah & Sarawak) datums. It is the reference frame used by JUPEM and is standard for modern Malaysian mapping, surveying and cadastral work.

How to convert GDM2000 coordinates

  1. Set FROM to the GDM2000 format you are pasting in — for example EPSG:3377 — GDM2000 Johor Grid.
  2. Set TO to the format you want out — for example Lat/Lng (DD) for WGS84, or MGRS.
  3. Type or paste your coordinates in the INPUT box, one coordinate per line.
  4. The OUTPUT box updates live. Tap COPY to copy every result.
When entering a GDM2000 grid coordinate, use the E…N form, for example -6793 E -4696 N, rather than a comma-separated pair.

Frequently asked questions

Is GDM2000 the same as WGS84?
Very close, but not identical. Both are based on the GRS80/WGS84 ellipsoid, so the difference is sub-metre for most work. For survey-grade accuracy, apply the EPSG transformation for the specific state grid.
Does this converter work offline?
Yes. The page works offline once loaded, and all conversion happens in your browser — coordinates are never uploaded.
Can I convert many coordinates at once?
Yes. Paste one coordinate per line and the whole batch converts at once.
